You read the numbers and the state of the close. You change nothing.

## Reports

- **Consolidated Statement**: the consolidated Profit and Loss and Balance Sheet for one root consolidation group and period, with the prior year beside it. Choose the **Fiscal Year**, **Fiscal Period** and **Consolidation Group**. A statement heading with a link opens **Statement Drill**, which shows what the heading is made of.
- **Period Close**: each period's status, what blocks its sign-off, and the next step.
- **My Work**: what the close is waiting for.

If the warehouse is not built, or setup is missing, a report says so. It does not show an empty table.

## The cards on this page

**Close Status**, **Trial Balances In**, **IC Pairs over Tolerance** and **Warehouse Freshness** describe the current period. Warehouse Freshness tells you whether the consolidated numbers include the latest approved changes.

## Excel

The Excel add-in reads the same numbers into a workbook with `=K.EPM(...)`. See *Excel add-in* under Concepts.

You see only the entities you are permitted to see. Ask the Close Lead if an entity is missing.
