You approve the group's records, review builds, and sign off and close each period.

## Approvals

Open a waiting record and use **Approve** or **Reject** in its **Actions** menu. This works on Consolidation Journal, Business Combination, Business Disposal, Group Exchange Rate, Ownership Period, Historical Equity Rate and IC Balance. A rejection needs a reason. The **Waiting for approval** shortcuts on this page count what is waiting.

Approving your own record follows **Self-Approval** in Close Settings: Blocked, or Allowed with reason. Until you declare it, every self-approval is refused.

## Builds

A trial balance submission, or a high-risk build that someone requested, waits in **Build Approval** as Pending Review. Approve it to run the build, or reject it. A build that an approval requested is approved at once, unless a build of the same scope is already waiting: the change then joins that build, which still needs your approval. The **Warehouse Freshness** card shows whether the consolidated numbers are current.

## Period close checklist

The **Period close** checklist on this page shows the current period, one line per check: the period and its setup, trial balances, ownership, rates, the checks, intercompany, open journals, equity rates waiting, the build and the sign-off. A line with a link opens the place where you fix it.

## TB Exceptions

When an entity has no trial balance for a period, add a **TB Exception** with the reason and submit it. Sign-off accepts it in place of the trial balance. The period must be Open.

## Sign-off and close (Period Close report)

Use the report's **Actions** menu:

1. **Run checks** (you or a Group Accountant).
2. **Sign off** the period on that run:
   - Green: sign.
   - Amber: type an acknowledgement of the warnings. A Green run is shown as Amber when intercompany pairs are over tolerance, or when statement headings above the commentary threshold have no commentary.
   - Red or Error: blocked, unless you override it with a reason. The override is recorded.
3. **Close period**.

A sign-off covers only the data its run checked. Run the checks again before you sign if, after they started, a trial balance or TB Exception was submitted or cancelled, an amount basis was set, or a journal, business combination or disposal, exchange rate, ownership period, equity rate or IC balance was approved or cancelled. Such a change also stops a later period's sign-off from counting until it is signed again. **Reject sign-off** sends the period back with a reason. **Reopen period** needs a reason; later signed periods then need signing again.

## Setup

**Konsol Setup** (EPM Admin and System Manager only) shows each setup area as done, partly done or not done, and links to where it is done.
